About Firefly: Gacha Meets Growtopia

Launched on February 19, 2024, Firefly Private Server was born from a simple dream: to blend the addictive gacha mechanics of games like Honkai: Star Rail, Genshin Impact, and Wuthering Waves with the creative sandbox freedom of Growtopia.

Our mission is bold—we're building the first true gacha experience within Growtopia. Imagine pulling for limited 5-star items, strategizing around pity systems, and collecting exclusive items—all while building, trading, and socializing in the world you love.

"We believe Growtopia's potential goes far beyond what's been explored. Firefly is our answer to that potential— a server where innovation meets nostalgia, where gacha excitement meets sandbox creativity."

Firefly's Revolutionary Features

Experience mechanics no other Growtopia server dares to implement.

Real Gacha System

Pull from rotating wish banners featuring limited 5-star items! We've implemented pity systems, guaranteed mechanics, and stunning pull animations.

5-Star Rarity Tier

Hunt for legendary 5-star items with unique sprites, effects, and prestige. Our custom rarity system adds depth and collection goals.

Pity & Guarantee System

Never feel cheated! Our gacha includes soft and hard pity systems ensuring you'll get your desired 5-star. Bad luck protection that respects your time!

50+ Custom Items

Discover an enormous library of custom-designed items, from seasonal cosmetics to game-changing tools. Expanded every update.

RPG Dungeon System

Battle monsters in dungeons to earn Monster Remnants. Build your character with gacha-like stats (Crit Rate, Crit DMG, ATK) and watch your 5-star items unleash their true effects!

Live Events

Participate in Fishing Events, Gift Of Odyssey, Automated Work System events and alot more!
